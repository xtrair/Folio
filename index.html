About

I've spent the last few years building interactive experiences and the systems that power them.

I work mostly on gameplay, mechanics and tooling — the kind of code that has to be fast, robust and easy to extend. My focus is craft: writing readable code, designing interfaces that get out of the way, and making sure every interaction feels intentional.

I work well alone and just as well in small teams. I'm always interested in projects where the goals are clear and the bar is high.
